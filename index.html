About ProStart Academy

What we are

ProStart Academy is a training community for people about to start their careers. We focus on the behaviour, ethics, and habits that help new staff settle into a workplace and be respected quickly.

What we have

We have sector-focused programmes, experienced facilitators, and practical sessions where you rehearse real workplace situations before you face them.

What we do

We run training sessions for graduates and NYSC leavers, and seminars for companies that want to prepare their new intake.

Our objective

To help new professionals blend into their new work environments, understand corporate ethics, and avoid the mistakes that slow down a career.

Our aim

To raise professionals who stay open to learning beyond the classroom, and who apply what they know in any organisation they join.
